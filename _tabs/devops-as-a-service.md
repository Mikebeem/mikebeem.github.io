## DevOps as a Service

DevOps as a Service is for organizations that want a stronger delivery platform
without immediately hiring a full-time DevOps or Platform Engineer. You get
senior support for tooling, workflows, and platform decisions in a form that
fits smaller teams or a limited number of hours.

### How does it work?

We start with a short intake and determine where the highest-impact improvement
work is. That can mean getting the basics right, taking ownership of recurring
maintenance, or driving improvements over time.

Typical examples include:

- **Setup and maintenance** of Azure DevOps, GitHub, or GitLab.
- **Optimization of CI/CD pipelines** for one or multiple projects.
- **Automation of workflows** to eliminate repetitive tasks.
- **Security checks and reporting** using tools like SonarCloud and GitLeaks.
- **Platform support** around Kubernetes, Argo CD, IaC, and standardization.

You choose the collaboration style that fits your team:

- **On demand** for questions, incidents, and targeted improvements.
- **Recurring support** for maintenance, optimization, and alignment.
- **Temporary part-time reinforcement** when you need continuity without a full-time role.

### Why choose DevOps as a Service?

- **No full-time hire required** while still bringing in senior expertise.
- **More calm in delivery and platform operations** because improvements keep moving.
- **Flexible support** that scales with the maturity and pace of your team.
- **A practical mix of advice and execution** instead of just a recommendation document.

### Who this is for

- Smaller organizations and scale-ups with a growing engineering process
- Teams that rely too much on manual work, tribal knowledge, or brittle scripts
- Organizations that want to professionalize delivery without overcommitting early
- Teams that want a trusted sparring partner for DevOps and platform decisions

### Customized collaboration

Every organization is different. That’s why my service is flexible and tailored
to your needs. Instead of heavy transformation programs or rigid packages, I work
as a strategic and hands-on partner who helps your team strengthen the platform
step by step. Together, we determine what level of support creates the most value.
